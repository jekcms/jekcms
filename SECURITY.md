# Security

If you find a security issue in jekcms, email **info@jekcms.com** with
"Security" in the subject line. Include the version (shown in the admin
panel), the steps to reproduce, and what an attacker could do with it.
Reports are accepted in English or Turkish.

Please do not open a public issue for security reports.

Confirmed findings are fixed, delivered through the signed update channel,
and announced in the [changelog](https://jekcms.com/changelog).

Contact details are also published in
[security.txt](https://jekcms.com/.well-known/security.txt). More about how
jekcms is secured: https://jekcms.com/security
